Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

Trucker Pay Protector LLC, a Florida limited liability company ("we," "us," "our"), makes the Trucker Pay Protector mobile app for Android and iPhone and runs truckerpayprotector.com (together, the "App"). This policy explains what information we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

The short version:

  • Your pay records are yours. We use them to run the App for you. If you're in our driver pay report (new sign-ups are in unless they turn it off, and anyone can turn it off any time), we also use rounded amounts from your paycheck checks in totals that never identify you.
  • We don't sell your information, and we never use it for other companies' ads.
  • We email you about your account and backups, and now and then with news about the App. You can turn off the news any time. Only we email you, and we never sell or share your email address.
  • We never share your data with your employer or carrier.
  • Location is read once, only when you tap to log an event. The App never tracks you in the background.
  • You can delete your account and data at any time.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Information you give us

  • Account information: your email address and password. You need an account to use the App. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We never see or store your password itself.
  • Work and pay information: the events you log (shifts, stops, loads, miles, hours, notes), your pay plan (pay rates and rules), your employer or carrier name if you enter it, and the pay the App calculates.
  • Documents you upload: photos or PDFs of pay stubs, settlement statements, rate confirmations, pay memos and similar documents. These can include personal details printed on them, such as your name, address, employee number, the last digits of bank or tax numbers, wages, deductions and year-to-date totals.
  • Questions you ask in the AI pay chat: the questions you tap or type in the App's pay chat, and the answers it gives.
  • Answers you report: if you tap "Report this answer" in the AI pay chat or "Report this review" on a paycheck review, we keep that question or review, the answer and any reason you type, on our servers, so a person can review it.
  • Problem reports (test version only): if you send one, your note, a picture of the screen (unless you turn it off), the pay numbers on it, your app version, phone model and your last few button taps.
  • Messages to us: anything you send when you contact support.
  • Waitlist sign-ups: if you join the waitlist on our website, your email address, driver type (company or owner-operator), how you are paid and, if you choose to answer, how you heard about us. The waitlist form is run by Google Forms.

1.2 Location
If you allow location access, the App reads your phone's precise location once, at the moment you tap to log an event, to fill in where the event happened. We store that location and the place name with the event. The App does not read your location in the background, when the App is closed, or between events, and it does not record your route. You can deny or turn off location access in your phone's settings at any time. The App still works, and you can type places in yourself.

1.3 Subscription information
Subscriptions are sold through the app store you use: Google Play on Android or the Apple App Store on iPhone. We receive your subscription status, plan, trial and renewal dates, and a purchase identifier from that app store, through our subscription service provider, RevenueCat. We never receive your card number or other payment details. Google or Apple handles those.

1.4 Technical information
When you use the App we receive basic technical information needed to run it securely, such as your device model, operating system version, App version, and error and crash reports. A crash report holds the type of error, technical details of where the App failed, and your account ID, so we can fix the bug. It doesn't include your pay records, documents, messages or location. To stop fake apps and automated abuse, the App may also ask Google Play Integrity (through Firebase App Check) to confirm that a request comes from our real App on a real device. Our servers (Google Firebase) automatically log IP addresses, request times and the result of that device check, for security and to limit abuse. We don't use advertising IDs (such as Android's Advertising ID or Apple's IDFA), we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we don't use ad or marketing trackers in the App or on our website.

1.5 Driver pay report (optional)
When you sign up, the App shows you our driver pay report on its own screen, which shows how often and by how much drivers' paychecks differ from their pay plans. It's on unless you turn it off on that screen, and saying no changes nothing else in the App. If you skip that screen, or you signed up before October 4, 2026, it's off unless you turn it on. You can turn it on or off any time in More > Profile > Privacy. Turning it off deletes the summaries you sent.

While it's on, each paycheck check you run sends us a short summary:

  • whether you're a company driver or owner-operator, and the month of the pay period;
  • the kinds of pay your plan uses (for example per mile, hourly or percent of the load), but never your rates; and
  • for each line the App checked, only its kind (earning, reimbursement or deduction), how it's counted (mile, hour, stop, load, percent or flat), whether it was right, short or over, and the difference rounded to the nearest dollar. If a line you logged was left off your check, that difference is the whole amount owed for it. If your check has a line you didn't log, we send only that it was over, with no amount.

The summary never includes your name, employer or carrier name, pay line names, rates, what you were paid on any line, documents, dates, locations, gross pay or net pay. We keep each summary with your account only so we can delete it when you ask.

We combine summaries into totals, such as how many drivers were paid less than their plan on miles in a month and by how much on average. We only show or share totals that combine at least 10 drivers, and never a single driver's summary. We may publish these totals, for example on our website, in a report or to the press, and show them to a possible buyer or investor. We don't try to identify anyone from these totals, and we don't allow anyone we share them with to try.

2. Where your information is stored

Most of your work records are stored on your phone. When backup is on, a copy of your records and documents is stored in our cloud account at Google Firebase (servers in the United States) so you can restore them on a new phone. Documents you upload for AI reading are stored in Firebase Storage.

3. How we use information

We use your information only to:

  • run the App's features: logging events, calculating pay under your pay plan, comparing expected and actual pay, and preparing summaries or messages you choose to share;
  • fill in the location of events you log;
  • read documents you upload with AI, to suggest pay-plan settings and read pay-stub lines for you to confirm;
  • answer questions you ask in the AI pay chat, and count them toward the monthly question limit;
  • review AI pay chat answers you report, to fix mistakes and improve the answers;
  • count how the App is used, in weekly totals that don't identify anyone (for example, how many drivers checked a paycheck that week), to improve the App and run our business;
  • if you're in the driver pay report, build the totals described in Section 1.5;
  • back up and restore your records;
  • manage your account, subscription and free trial;
  • respond to you when you contact us;
  • keep the App secure, prevent abuse and fix errors;
  • send account, sign-in, backup, billing and policy emails, which are part of the App;
  • now and then, email you news and tips about the App, and about the launch if you joined the waitlist. Only we send these. We never sell or share your email address, we never use your pay records, documents or location to decide what we send, and you can turn these off any time; and
  • follow the law.

We don't use your pay records, documents or location to target ads, and we don't sell them or use them to build profiles for anyone else. We don't use your documents or pay records to train AI models.

4. AI features (Anthropic)

The App uses AI in three places:

  • Document reading. When you ask the App to read a document, our server sends that document (or the relevant pages).
  • AI pay chat (formerly Gut Check). When you ask a question, our server sends your question and a short written summary of your own pay plan and the pay period you're asking about. It does not send your documents or photos.
  • Paycheck review. When you tap "Review my paycheck", our server sends the differences the App found on that paycheck, your pay plan's rules, and the few lines of your own pay documents that bear on them. It does not send whole documents, photos or your name.

In each case the data goes to Anthropic, the company that makes the Claude AI models, through Anthropic's business API. Anthropic processes it only to return the result to us, as our service provider. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, Anthropic does not use this data to train its models, and it keeps API data for no more than 30 days, except where the law requires longer or where Anthropic's automated safety systems flag content for review (in which case it may be kept up to 2 years). Only use these features for information you are comfortable sending to our service provider.

5. Who we share information with

We share information only in these cases:

  • Service providers that run parts of the App for us, under contracts that limit how they can use it: Google (Firebase sign-in, database, file storage, server functions, crash reporting, app integrity checks, and Google Play billing), Apple (App Store billing, for iPhone users), Anthropic (AI document reading, Section 4), RevenueCat (subscription status), GitHub (hosts our website), Google Forms (waitlist), and an email service that sends our emails for us.
  • When you choose to share. For example, when you send a pay summary or message from the App using your phone's share button, it goes to whoever you choose.
  • Legal reasons: if required by law, court order or subpoena, or to protect the rights, safety or property of our users, the public or us. Where the law allows, we will try to tell you before handing over your data.
  • Business transfer: if our company is merged, acquired or sold, your information may transfer to the new owner, who must follow this policy or give you notice and a choice.

We never share your information with your employer, motor carrier or their payroll providers, unless you send it to them yourself.

6. How long we keep information

  • While your account is open: we keep your records and documents so the App works and so you have your pay history. Old pay records can matter in a pay dispute, so we don't delete them unless you do.
  • When you delete something: it's removed from the App right away and from our cloud copy at your next backup sync.
  • Answers you report: kept while your account is open, so we can review them and check our fixes.
  • Security logs (IP addresses, request times, device check results): kept about 30 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Driver pay report summaries: kept while the report is on and your account is open. We delete them when you turn the report off or delete your account. Totals already combined from 10 or more drivers stay, because they can't be traced back to you.
  • Usage totals: these don't identify anyone, so we keep them as business records.
  • Unsubscribes: if you unsubscribe, we keep your email address on a do-not-email list so we don't send you news emails again.
  • Problem reports: deleted after 90 days, including our email copies.
  • Crash reports: kept for up to 90 days, then deleted automatically. After you delete your account, any crash reports already sent age out on that same schedule.
  • When you delete your account: we delete your account, records, documents, reported answers and locations from our active systems within 30 days. Copies in backup systems are overwritten within 90 days. Deleting in the App also clears everything the App saved on that phone. If you use the App on more than one phone, clear the others by uninstalling it there.
  • Switching accounts on one phone: when a different account signs in, the App clears the phone first. One account's records are never moved into another account.
  • What we keep after deletion: a record that the account was deleted and when; subscription and transaction records needed for taxes and accounting (up to 7 years); and anything we're legally required to keep or need for a legal claim. These are kept only for those purposes.
  • Waitlist: kept until the App launches publicly plus 12 months, or until you ask us to remove you, whichever comes first.
  • Data on your phone stays there until you delete it, delete your account in the App (that clears the phone right away) or uninstall the App. If you delete your account by email or on our website, the App on your phone is not cleared; uninstall it to remove what's there.

7. Your choices and rights

For everyone, wherever you live, you can:

  • see and change your records in the App;
  • get a copy of your records: share any day's report from the App, or email us and we'll send you a copy of everything we hold about you;
  • delete individual records or documents in the App;
  • delete your account and data: in the App at More > Delete my account, or on the web at truckerpayprotector.com/delete-account, or by emailing us;
  • turn off location in your phone settings;
  • turn the driver pay report on or off in More > Profile > Privacy;
  • turn off news emails or leave the waitlist, using the link in any of those emails or by emailing us. Account, sign-in, backup, billing and policy emails still come while you have an account; and
  • ask us what information we have about you, or ask us to correct it.

Some state laws (for example, in California, Colorado, Connecticut, Florida, Minnesota, Oregon, Texas and Virginia) give residents specific privacy rights, such as the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of their data, and to opt out of sale, targeted advertising and profiling. We don't sell data, do targeted advertising or make decisions about you by profiling, and we offer the rights above to everyone. To make a request, email support@truckerpayprotector.com from your account email. We'll answer within 45 days. If we deny your request, you can appeal by replying to our answer with "Appeal" in the subject line. We'll respond to an appeal within 60 days. We won't treat you differently for using your privacy rights.

Sensitive information. Precise location and pay information are treated as sensitive under some state laws. We collect precise location only after you allow it in your phone's permission prompt, and we use both only for the purposes in this policy.

8. Security

We protect your information with encryption in transit (HTTPS) and at rest (Google Firebase), access rules that let only your account read your cloud data, and limited internal access. No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a security breach that affects your personal information, we'll notify you and regulators as the law requires.

9. Children

The App is for adults 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

10. Our website

Our website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which logs visitor IP addresses for security. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads. The site doesn't use cookies, analytics or ad trackers. The waitlist form opens on Google Forms, which has its own privacy policy.

11. Changes to this policy

We'll post any changes here and update the effective date. For important changes, we'll tell you in the App or by email before they take effect. We won't use information we already have in a materially new way without your consent.
