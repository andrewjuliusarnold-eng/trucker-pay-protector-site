Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026

Trucker Pay Protector LLC, a Florida limited liability company ("we," "us," "our"), makes the Trucker Pay Protector mobile app for Android and iPhone and runs truckerpayprotector.com (together, the "App"). This policy explains what information we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

The short version:

  • Your pay records are yours. We use them only to run the App for you.
  • We don't sell your information, and we don't use it for ads.
  • We never share your data with your employer or carrier.
  • Location is read once, only when you tap to log an event. The App never tracks you in the background.
  • You can delete your account and data at any time.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Information you give us

  • Account information: your email address and password. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We never see or store your password itself.
  • Work and pay information: the events you log (shifts, stops, loads, miles, hours, notes), your pay plan (pay rates and rules), your employer or carrier name if you enter it, and the pay the App calculates.
  • Documents you upload: photos or PDFs of pay stubs, settlement statements, rate confirmations, pay memos and similar documents. These can include personal details printed on them, such as your name, address, employee number, the last digits of bank or tax numbers, wages, deductions and year-to-date totals.
  • Messages to us: anything you send when you contact support.
  • Waitlist sign-ups: if you join the waitlist on our website, your email address, driver type (company or owner-operator) and how you are paid. The waitlist form is run by Google Forms.

1.2 Location
If you allow location access, the App reads your phone's precise location once, at the moment you tap to log an event, to fill in where the event happened. We store that location and the place name with the event. The App does not read your location in the background, when the App is closed, or between events, and it does not record your route. You can deny or turn off location access in your phone's settings at any time. The App still works, and you can type places in yourself.

1.3 Subscription information
Subscriptions are sold through the app store you use: Google Play on Android or the Apple App Store on iPhone. We receive your subscription status, plan, trial and renewal dates, and a purchase identifier from that app store, through our subscription service provider, RevenueCat. We never receive your card number or other payment details. Google or Apple handles those.

1.4 Technical information
When you use the App we receive basic technical information needed to run it securely, such as your device model, operating system version, App version, and error and crash reports. Our servers (Google Firebase) automatically log IP addresses and request times for security. We don't use advertising IDs (such as Android's Advertising ID or Apple's IDFA), we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we don't use ad or marketing trackers in the App or on our website.

2. Where your information is stored

Most of your work records are stored on your phone. When you sign in and backup is on, a copy of your records and documents is stored in our cloud account at Google Firebase (servers in the United States) so you can restore them on a new phone. Documents you upload for AI reading are stored in Firebase Storage.

3. How we use information

We use your information only to:

  • run the App's features: logging events, calculating pay under your pay plan, comparing expected and actual pay, and preparing summaries or messages you choose to share;
  • fill in the location of events you log;
  • read documents you upload with AI, to suggest pay-plan settings and read pay-stub lines for you to confirm;
  • back up and restore your records;
  • manage your account, subscription and free trial;
  • respond to you when you contact us;
  • keep the App secure, prevent abuse and fix errors;
  • tell you about the App launch if you joined the waitlist, and send important account, billing or policy notices; and
  • follow the law.

We don't use your pay records, documents or location to target ads, and we don't sell them or use them to build profiles for anyone else. We don't use your documents or pay records to train AI models.

4. AI document reading (Anthropic)

When you ask the App to read a document, our server sends that document (or the relevant pages) to Anthropic, the company that makes the Claude AI models, through Anthropic's business API. Anthropic processes it only to return the reading to us, as our service provider. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, Anthropic does not use this data to train its models, and it keeps API data for no more than 30 days, except where the law requires longer or where Anthropic's automated safety systems flag content for review (in which case it may be kept up to 2 years). Only use AI reading for documents you are comfortable sending to our service provider.

5. Who we share information with

We share information only in these cases:

  • Service providers that run parts of the App for us, under contracts that limit how they can use it: Google (Firebase sign-in, database, file storage, server functions, crash reporting, and Google Play billing), Apple (App Store billing, for iPhone users), Anthropic (AI document reading, Section 4), RevenueCat (subscription status), GitHub (hosts our website), and Google Forms (waitlist).
  • When you choose to share. For example, when you send a pay summary or message from the App using your phone's share button, it goes to whoever you choose.
  • Legal reasons: if required by law, court order or subpoena, or to protect the rights, safety or property of our users, the public or us. Where the law allows, we will try to tell you before handing over your data.
  • Business transfer: if our company is merged, acquired or sold, your information may transfer to the new owner, who must follow this policy or give you notice and a choice.

We never share your information with your employer, motor carrier or their payroll providers, unless you send it to them yourself.

6. How long we keep information

  • While your account is open: we keep your records and documents so the App works and so you have your pay history. Old pay records can matter in a pay dispute, so we don't delete them unless you do.
  • When you delete something: it's removed from the App right away and from our cloud copy at your next backup sync.
  • When you delete your account: we delete your account, records, documents and locations from our active systems within 30 days. Copies in backup systems are overwritten within 90 days.
  • What we keep after deletion: a record that the account was deleted and when; subscription and transaction records needed for taxes and accounting (up to 7 years); and anything we're legally required to keep or need for a legal claim. These are kept only for those purposes.
  • Waitlist: kept until the App launches publicly plus 12 months, or until you ask us to remove you, whichever comes first.
  • Data on your phone stays there until you delete it or uninstall the App.

7. Your choices and rights

For everyone, wherever you live, you can:

  • see and change your records in the App;
  • download your records (export) from the App;
  • delete individual records or documents in the App;
  • delete your account and data: in the App at More > Account > Delete account, or on the web at truckerpayprotector.com/delete-account, or by emailing us;
  • turn off location in your phone settings;
  • leave the waitlist or stop non-essential emails using the link in the email or by emailing us; and
  • ask us what information we have about you, or ask us to correct it.

Some state laws (for example, in California, Colorado, Connecticut, Florida, Minnesota, Oregon, Texas and Virginia) give residents specific privacy rights, such as the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of their data, and to opt out of sale, targeted advertising and profiling. We don't sell data, do targeted advertising or make decisions about you by profiling, and we offer the rights above to everyone. To make a request, email support@truckerpayprotector.com from your account email. We'll answer within 45 days. If we deny your request, you can appeal by replying to our answer with "Appeal" in the subject line. We'll respond to an appeal within 60 days. We won't treat you differently for using your privacy rights.

Sensitive information. Precise location and pay information are treated as sensitive under some state laws. We collect precise location only after you allow it in your phone's permission prompt, and we use both only for the purposes in this policy.

8. Security

We protect your information with encryption in transit (HTTPS) and at rest (Google Firebase), access rules that let only your account read your cloud data, and limited internal access. No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a security breach that affects your personal information, we'll notify you and regulators as the law requires.

9. Children

The App is for adults 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

10. Our website

Our website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which logs visitor IP addresses for security. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads. The site doesn't use cookies, analytics or ad trackers. The waitlist form opens on Google Forms, which has its own privacy policy.

11. Changes to this policy

We'll post any changes here and update the effective date. For important changes, we'll tell you in the App or by email before they take effect. We won't use information we already have in a materially new way without your consent.
