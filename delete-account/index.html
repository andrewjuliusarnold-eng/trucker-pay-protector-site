Delete Your Account

Trucker Pay Protector, by Trucker Pay Protector LLC

You can delete your Trucker Pay Protector account and data at any time, whether or not you still have the app installed.

Option 1: In the app

Open Trucker Pay Protector, then go to More > Delete my account. Confirm, and your account is scheduled for deletion right away. This also clears everything the app saved on that phone.

Option 2: By email (no app needed)

Don't have the app anymore, or would rather not open it? Type your email below. We'll send a link to delete your account. It works for 24 hours.

Prefer email? Write to support@truckerpayprotector.com with the subject Delete my account and the email address your account uses instead.

Deleting by email or with the form above doesn't clear the app on your phone. Uninstall it to remove what's there.

What gets deleted

Either way, here's what happens:

  • Your account, logged events, pay plan, uploaded documents, and any locations you logged are deleted from our active systems within 30 days.
  • Copies kept in backup systems are overwritten within 90 days.
  • We keep a record that the account existed and was deleted, and any subscription or transaction records we're required to keep for taxes and accounting (up to 7 years). These are kept only for that purpose.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel that separately in your app store (Google Play or Apple App Store), or the app's subscription link will take you there.

Full details are in our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

The app is still in beta and isn't publicly released yet. If you're not sure whether you have an account, email the address above and we'll check.
