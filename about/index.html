Why I built this

Trucker Pay Protector reads your own pay memo, lease, or rate cons and goes through each paystub you add, line by line. Detention, layover, mileage, all of it. Here's why I built it.

I'm Andrew. I grew up in Ohio, I live in Florida now with my wife and our baby, and I drive over the road as a company driver.

For a long time I was leaving money on the table. My pay plan came as a whole packet of pay codes, rates and rules, and I'll be straight with you: I couldn't make sense of all of it. So I logged my miles and hours like everybody else and figured the check was right.

I got tired of guessing. I built myself a simple app to track my work against that packet, line by line. The first thing it showed me was pay I qualified for and had never been writing down. For me, that came to about $500 a week. That's my number on my pay plan. Most drivers will find less, and many will find their pay is already right. The app hasn't launched, so there's no typical number yet.

Nobody ever walked me through it, and I didn't know to claim it. That still makes me mad.

Then I started asking around. I wasn't the only one, and other drivers wanted to know how I did it. I'd built the app just for my own pay, so now I'm building it for everyone: company drivers and owner-operators, using your own pay documents.

That's Trucker Pay Protector. It doesn't change your pay plan. It helps you understand the one you already have and check each paystub you add against it.

- Andrew, founder
