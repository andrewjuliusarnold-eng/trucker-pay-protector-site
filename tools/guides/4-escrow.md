# What escrow is, and how to track it

Escrow is money a carrier holds back from your settlement. It is kept to pay for things later, like damage or unpaid costs. Your lease says how it works, and if and when you get back what is left after you leave.

## What your lease may say

Look for these things:

- How much is taken each week
- The total the carrier holds. Once you reach it, the weekly take may stop.
- What the carrier may use it for
- Whether it earns interest
- How often the carrier tells you the balance
- When and how you get it back after you leave

## How to track it

1. Make a list. One line for each week.
2. Write down how much was taken out for escrow.
3. Write down anything the carrier says it used.
4. Keep a running total.

Made-up example:
Week 1: $100.00 in. Total $100.00.
Week 2: $100.00 in. Total $200.00.
Week 3: $100.00 in, $50.00 used for a repair. Total $250.00.

5. Compare your total with the carrier's statement each time you get one.

## Why tracking matters

When you leave, you want to know what you should get back. If you kept your own list, you can check the carrier's number.

## If the numbers do not match

Write down each week, your total and the carrier's total. Ask the carrier's settlement office. Keep a copy.
