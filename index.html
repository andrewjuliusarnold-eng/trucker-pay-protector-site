How it works
1

Upload your documents

Your pay memo, lease, or rate confirmations, plus a couple of recent paystubs or settlements.

2

Confirm your pay plan

The app drafts your exact rules from your own documents and asks you to confirm each one, card by card.

3

Log your day

One tap per stop, delay, or event — the same way you'd note it for your own records.

4

Check every check

Each paycheck or settlement gets matched line by line against what you logged and what your documents promise.

Who this is for

Company drivers

Per-mile, hourly, or activity-code pay plans with a lot of small line items to keep straight.

Leased owner-operators

Settlement deductions, escrow, and percentage-of-revenue math checked against your own lease.

Own-authority owner-operators

Every rate confirmation checked against what actually lands in your account.

Trucker Pay Protector checks your pay against your own documents. It isn't legal, tax, or payroll advice, and your employer's or broker's records remain the official ones.
