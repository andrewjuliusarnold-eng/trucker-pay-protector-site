How it works

Four easy steps.

  1. 1

    Add your papers

    Show the app your pay papers and one recent paystub.

  2. 2

    Say yes to your plan

    The app writes down your pay rules. You check each one.

  3. 3

    Log your day

    Tap once for each stop, wait or event.

  4. 4

    Check your pay

    The app checks each line. It shows any line that does not match, and why.

Questions

Good to know

Is this legal, tax or payroll advice?

No. The app shows what your papers say and what your paystub says. If something looks very wrong, talk to your payroll office, your state labor office or a lawyer.

Does it promise I get money back?

No. It shows you which lines do not match, and where each number came from. It does not file anything or call anyone for you.

Will my employer or carrier know I use it?

Not from us. We never contact your employer, your carrier or their payroll company. We only share what you choose to send.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. Right now the app is for Android phones. The iPhone app is coming soon.

Who is it for?

Company drivers and owner-operators, leased or with their own authority. The app asks which one you are when you set it up.

Is my info sold or used for ads?

No. We do not sell your info, show ads or use your pay to build ad profiles. Read more in our Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my account?

You can delete it in the app. You can also use our delete account page.

How do I get help?

Email us at support@truckerpayprotector.com.
