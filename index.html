FAQ
Is this legal, tax, or payroll advice?

No. Everything the app shows is an estimate based on your pay plan and the documents you give it. It doesn't interpret your contract as a matter of law, and it isn't a substitute for talking to your payroll department, a wage-and-hour agency, or a lawyer if something looks seriously wrong.

Does it guarantee I'll get money back?

No. The app shows you the difference between what your documents say you're owed and what you were actually paid, and where that number came from. It doesn't promise any recovery, doesn't file anything, and doesn't contact your employer or carrier for you. What you do with the information is always your decision.

Does it tell my employer or carrier I'm using it?

Never. We don't contact your employer, motor carrier, or their payroll providers, and we never share your data with them unless you send it to them yourself.

What if I'm a company driver, not an owner-operator?

Both will be supported at launch. Company drivers set up a pay plan from their pay memo; owner-operators add their lease or rate confirmations and settlement deductions. The app asks which you are during setup.

Is my data sold or used for ads?

No. We don't sell your information, don't use it for advertising, and don't build profiles with it. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.
