Beta Tester Agreement

Effective date: October 16, 2026

Thanks for helping test Trucker Pay Protector before it goes on sale. This short agreement is between you and Trucker Pay Protector LLC ("we," "us"). By replying "I agree" to our invitation (by email or text), tapping "I agree", or installing a test version, you accept it.

1. It's a test version

The test version (the "Beta") is unfinished. Expect bugs, wrong numbers, crashes and features that change or disappear.

Any difference the app shows between your log and your paycheck is preliminary. Check it yourself against your paystub and pay documents before you contact payroll or make any money decision based on it. Your payroll or carrier has the final word.

Don't use it while driving. Tap or type only when you're stopped (49 CFR 392.80 and 392.82).

Your account and everything you enter carry over to the released app. We won't reset or delete tester data. It's still test software, so a bug could lose something: keep your own copies of anything important.

2. Your data

You can use your real shifts, miles, pay agreements and paystubs. We protect them as our Privacy Policy describes.

Our Privacy Policy covers everything you put in the Beta. Only upload your own pay documents. Don't upload anyone else's.

3. Free while it lasts

The Beta is free. You won't be charged for anything during the test. When the app goes on sale, you get the founding price too. Start within 60 days of launch and you keep it as long as your plan stays active without a break. You aren't paid for testing, and testing doesn't make you our employee or contractor.

4. Feedback

We'd like your honest feedback: what's broken, what's confusing, what's missing. You let us use any feedback or ideas you send, for free and however we like. We won't publish your name, pay details or documents without your written OK.

5. Keep it between us (lightly)

Feel free to tell people you're testing it. Please don't post screenshots or recordings of the Beta publicly until it's on sale. Test screens change, and pay documents in the background can expose your own information. Never post a screenshot that shows your employer's name or paperwork.

6. Reviews

The Beta is free, and we're thankful, but that doesn't buy a review. If you leave a store review after launch, make it your honest opinion, and mention that you were a beta tester who got the founding price.

7. Ending

You can stop testing any time: just uninstall. Uninstalling doesn't delete your account or backed-up records; to delete them, use Delete account in the app or truckerpayprotector.com/delete-account. We can end the Beta, or your access to it, at any time. Sections 4, 6 and 8 still apply after the Beta ends.

The Terms of Service apply to the Beta, including the disclaimer, the limit on liability and the arbitration agreement. Because the Beta is free and unfinished, it's provided "as is" with no warranty of any kind, and our liability for it is limited to $100. You must be 18 or older and in the United States to test.
